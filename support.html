Things people ask

Where's my history kept?

On your iPhone. If iCloud is on, a copy also sits in your own iCloud account so your other iPhones and your Watch show the same count. There's no Pause account and I can't see any of it.

I got a new iPhone.

Sign in with the same Apple Account and open Pause, and your history comes back through iCloud. You can also save a copy yourself at any time: Settings › Export history as CSV.

My Watch (or my other iPhone) shows a different number.

Make sure both are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, then open Pause on each one. Settings tells you whether sync is working.

The widget is stuck on an old number.

Open Pause once and the widget catches up.

I logged one by mistake.

Go to History. Tap an entry to change its time or reason, or swipe it to the right to delete it.

When does the day reset?

At midnight wherever you are. Older days stay in History.
