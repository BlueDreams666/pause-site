Pause

A cigarette counter for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Download on theApp Store

Free on the App Store.

Tap once when you light one. The main screen shows today's count next to a daily limit you set yourself, and History keeps the time and the reason for every one, so after a couple of weeks you can see when in the day you smoke the most.

It also works from your wrist and from a widget. If you have more than one iPhone, iCloud keeps the count the same on all of them.

You don't need an account, there are no ads, and it won't tell you smoking is bad. You know.
