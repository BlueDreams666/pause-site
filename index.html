Pause

A cigarette counter for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Download on theApp Store

Free on the App Store.

Two taps when you light one: one to log it, one to say why. The main screen shows today's count next to a daily limit you set yourself, and History keeps the time and the reason for every one, so after a couple of weeks you can see when in the day you smoke the most.

You can log from your wrist too, and a widget keeps today's count on your Home Screen. If you have more than one iPhone, iCloud keeps the count the same on all of them.

You don't need an account, there are no ads, and it won't tell you smoking is bad. You know.
