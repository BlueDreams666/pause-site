Privacy

In effect from October 28, 2026. I'm Tai Maor and I make Pause on my own.

Pause doesn't collect anything about you. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it never talks to a server of mine, because I don't run one.

On your iPhone and Watch

Everything you log (each entry, its reason, your daily limit, your settings) is stored on your iPhone, and on your Apple Watch if you use one. The widget can only read two things from the app: today's count and your limit. If iCloud Backup is on for your phone, this data is part of that backup like any other app's.

iCloud

With iCloud turned on, Pause keeps a copy of your history in your own iCloud account so that your iPhones and Watch agree on the count. Apple runs iCloud; I have no access to it.

Exports

Settings › Export history as CSV makes a file only when you tap it. You decide where it goes.

Tips

If Pause offers optional tips, you pay for them through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and your Apple Account, and I never see your name, email address or card.

Deleting things

An entry you delete in History disappears from all your devices. Deleting the app deletes its data from that device.

Kids

Pause is made for adults and isn't aimed at children.

If this changes

I'll update this page and the date at the top. If Pause ever starts collecting something, the app will tell you before it does.
